The lanes, heard again and again

Each row plays one sound the way a carry does, many times over. Play it before and after and listen to the twentieth one, not the first.

level: loudness over the whole run (RMS dBFS) · peak: loudest sample (dBFS) · bright: share of the energy above 4 kHz · tail: the last quarter of the run against the first, in dB (below zero means the ear is spared as it repeats)
