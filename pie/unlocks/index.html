Exactly One Pie · unlocks

What the game unlocks

A few mechanics, each one sentence long. They unlock by weight: rooms read, wonders kept, cards turned, rhythms found and carries finished each count toward them, reading most of all. Any of them can be tried for a visit from its page, and nothing anyone has is ever taken away.

The unlocks

  • Two registers at once

    Choose two registers at the desk and the lanes answer in both, the later one asked first.

    Eight to unlock: rooms read in any chapter, one each; wonders kept in the almanac, one each; rhythms found in the lanes, two each; carries finished, half each (up to twelve).

  • The rhythm groove

    Rhythms you have found play softly under every carry.

    One to unlock: rhythms found in the lanes, one each.

  • In the pocket

    Steps, hops and throws on the beat of a chapter's found rhythm count a little toward low magic.

    Four to unlock: rhythms found in the lanes, two each; rooms read in any chapter, half each; carries finished, a quarter each (up to twelve).
