A small game · the lanes of Bonk City

Exactly One Pie

Someone is destined to spill exactly one pie. The prophecy has been very clear about the number and no help at all about the name.

Through the lanes of Bonk City, someone in a hat is carrying one, level, in both hands. The city streams down at them: bronze posts, flour sacks, pane-snails, feast clerks with their DAMAGE slips. Whoever it is does not go the way the city goes. They step across it, a lane at a time.

It is not always him under the hat. A title like that floats, the way the hat does, and settles on whoever is nearest: the figure in plain lines, the porter from the parcel gate, a feast clerk, the Genie between seasons, now and then a clown. At a change of scene it may move on.

And clowns love WAP. That is what Bonk City calls a pie in the face, after the sound it makes. They will come for the pie. Let them have it, and the pie on the plate is somehow no smaller.

The prophecy always holds. What changes is who carried it, how far, and how many clowns went home happy.
