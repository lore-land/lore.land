A small game · the lanes of Bonk City

Exactly One Pie

Someone is destined to spill exactly one pie. The prophecy has been very clear about the number and no help at all about the name.

Through the lanes of Bonk City, a figure in a hat is carrying one, level, in both hands. The city streams down at him: bronze posts, flour sacks, pane-snails, feast clerks with their DAMAGE slips. He does not go the way the city goes. He steps across it, a lane at a time.

The prophecy always holds. What changes is how far he carries it.
