A small game · the lanes of Bonk City

Exactly One Pie

Someone is destined to spill exactly one pie. The prophecy has been very clear about the number and no help at all about the name.

Someone in a hat carries it, level, up the lanes of Bonk City while the city streams down at them. They never go the way the city goes. They step across it, a lane at a time.

The prophecy always holds. What changes is who carried it, how far, and how many clowns went home happy.

What everything is

In the lanes

The city comes in rows, and every row leaves a way through, never more than a step from the last. Every step tilts the pie, hurried steps most; let it settle. The level under the screen shows how far it has tipped.

The pie in hand stays whole because an aura keeps replenishing it, and the aura needs two ingredients: flour for the crust, boonberries for the filling. Every slice you throw draws on both; a slice that reaches nobody costs more. Run either dry and no new slices come, and a thin pie tips more easily. The two levels at the foot of the screen show what is left.

~ Slows you

Not a spill, but not nothing.

  • Bag of flourCrust for the aura. It also slows the carry and pushes you back in the story; the track under the score shows how far along you are. Throw a slice at a bag and it bursts into a pile.
  • Pile of flourA little crust, a little slower, no setback. And a ramp: a slice thrown over a pile is lofted clean over whatever is next. Piles in a row loft it further.
  • PuddleThe pie slides; it tilts a good deal.

✦ Go toward

These glow.

  • ClownClowns love WAP: a pie in the eye. Step into one, or throw: the needle sweeps from the pie, and when it lines up with a clown’s face the ring turns gold. Dead centre, off a wall, or off a ramp is Perfect and counts twice.
  • BoonberriesTeal and amber, lit from within: the filling for the aura. Step through them.
  • BoofCrosses the lanes on her own business. Brush past her and the pie steadies.
  • Off switchMr. BaneWAP always leaves one. Step on it, or hit it with a slice, and he is foiled. His rented wind drifts slices too.

◎ Two of the quinary five

They come and go, never together, and they talk.

  • Mr. BoonWAPA sentient pie who means well. While he holds court, clowns flock, every pie finds an eye, he pours filling back into your aura and catches any slice you waste, and he says one true thing about the land.
  • Mr. BaneWAPHis foil. A rented wind, a tax on every step, the lanes re-let. Foiled, he says more than he means to.

⌒ Under the hat

It is not always him. The hat settles on someone, and at a change of place it may move on.
