The book, from the first page

Every chapter, in order

New chapters are set on the 13th and 26th of each month. Chapter 16 is set for 13 November.

Book One · The Unspent Wish

  1. Dawn in Boon.land in which a dog finds an egg the maps will not admit
  2. The First Echo in which the grove answers a beat late, and will not swear to it
  3. Signals in the Sand in which two roads claim the same step
  4. Crosswind Council in which keys are filed smaller, and none of them fit
  5. The Quiet Rift in which two slips try to be one harvest
  6. Lanterns of Bonk City in which glass shows a light it does not own
  7. Blueberry Oath in which an oath is paid in fruit
  8. Songs of the Watering Eye in which water keeps a face without stealing it
  9. Shards of Nine Honks in which the ninth honk is missing, and warms
  10. Bone.land Resonance in which a hall tells the truth with its weight
  11. Paradox Bloom in which a garden blooms before its seed arrives
  12. The Last Confluence in which rivers meet and remember their names
  13. The Shell-Shaped Door in which the last door is shaped like a shell, and stays open

Book Two · Wonder About Pi(e)

  1. The Thursday Nobody Kept in which a day goes missing from the week, and the field keeps it anyway
  2. Pupil, Parcel, or Door in which one stranger is written down three ways, and all three are right
