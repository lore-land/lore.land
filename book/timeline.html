The book, from the first page

Every chapter, in order

Two complete books of thirteen chambers — twenty-six chapters in all.

Book One · The Unspent Wish

  1. Dawn in Boon.land in which a dog finds an egg the maps will not admit
  2. The First Echo in which the grove answers a beat late, and will not swear to it
  3. Signals in the Sand in which two roads claim the same step
  4. Crosswind Council in which keys are filed smaller, and none of them fit
  5. The Quiet Rift in which two slips try to be one harvest
  6. Lanterns of Bonk City in which glass shows a light it does not own
  7. Blueberry Oath in which an oath is paid in fruit
  8. Songs of the Watering Eye in which water keeps a face without stealing it
  9. Shards of Nine Honks in which the ninth honk is missing, and warms
  10. Bone.land Resonance in which a hall tells the truth with its weight
  11. Paradox Bloom in which a garden blooms before its seed arrives
  12. The Last Confluence in which rivers meet and remember their names
  13. The Shell-Shaped Door in which the last door is shaped like a shell, and stays open

Book Two · Wonder About Pi(e)

  1. The Thursday Nobody Kept in which a day goes missing from the week, and the field keeps it anyway
  2. Pupil, Parcel, or Door in which one stranger is written down three ways, and all three are right
  3. The Second Place in which Boof wears the chosen title for one supper, and the figure dries plates behind the curtain beside the unnamed washer-up
  4. Ledger of Unasked Questions in which clerks file answers before anyone asks a question, and an unspent coincidence turns up as a form nobody owns
  5. Flour in the Bell-Tower in which one misplaced step fills the belfry with flour, and the city’s loudest bell strikes in white silence
  6. The Priced Window in which glaziers chalk rising tariffs onto every pane while pane-snails slowly graze the prices back to clear glass
  7. Two Springs Under the Sill in which two canal gates share a swollen sill by taking turns every third chime, while Mr. BaneWAP tastes bitter jam
  8. At the Nose of the Work in which the whole city is read from fourteen inches above the cobblestones, where crumbs tell what tall tables hide
  9. Through the Chute With No Receipt in which parcels slide down a copper chute as descriptions of someone else’s bundle while receipts blow up the return duct
  10. Rehearsal of the Night School in which grown-ups gather by tallow light to measure a circle with string and dividers that refuses to close evenly
  11. Four Windows in One Wall in which four masking shutters prove four opposite laws until two open at once and show the unbroken courtyard
  12. The Warm Hearth Before the Feast in which every unfinished ingredient from the season simmers under one bakehouse vault while hungry clowns wait politely outside
  13. The Feast of the Uneven Slice in which Bonk City’s Great Table gathers every voice, exactly one slice spills, and the ring closes whole without resetting
