Thirteen chambers, one egg carried through. Chapter One opens the furrow; later installments follow the heat — cell, key, turn, receipt — without flattening the tributaries.

  1. 01Dawn in Boon.landAt dawn, Boof digs a warm egg out of a frozen furrow—and every map pretends it was never there.
  2. 02The First EchoThe grove repeats the egg a beat late — so there always seems to be a second one just out of sight.
  3. 03Signals in the SandTwo old routes want to be one turn. The egg is the only witness that both were true.
  4. 04Crosswind CouncilKeys may be filed smaller, never grown in secret. No key yet covers the egg.
  5. 05The Quiet RiftTwo receipts cannot both post. The rift is the song of that disagreement.
  6. 06Lanterns of Bonk CityImpact becomes glass: lanterns that show light without claiming to be it.
  7. 07Blueberry OathA key given in public, paid in fruit, with the egg as witness.
  8. 08Songs of the Watering EyeA well that folds every face it has held. The egg's heat names a debt in the water.
  9. 09Shards of Nine HonksEight honks scatter. The ninth was never sounded; its shard is the egg.
  10. 10Bone.land ResonanceThe small constitution — the spine that holds when the veil shakes.
  11. 11Paradox BloomBloom before seed: the egg is the seed that arrives late and is right anyway.
