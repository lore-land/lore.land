At the map-house · the round map

The map that will not lie flat

The map-house keeps one map for things that will not lie flat: every thread, mark, voice, lean, wonder and material in the book, folded onto a disk the way frill-lichen folds onto a stone, with more edge than room. Whatever you look at opens out at the middle; everything else folds toward the rim and never falls off it. Touch a place to turn it to the middle, or ask the map in Spw, and watch the question run through the chapters, and through the cards below.
