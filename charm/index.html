Ask the land for a charm

A charm is something small you would like the land to hold: a wonder you hope to meet, a rhythm for the lanes, a recipe to keep beside the story, a line kept for someone you love, without their name. Write it on a card at autonomous.feedback, the post the Scriptorium reads.

Write a charm Read the post as an address

What a charm can be

  • A charm for someone · a line, a berry or a light the land could keep for them, unnamed
  • Something you would like to meet · a wonder, a rhythm or a sound for the lanes, a recipe beside the story
  • A name for something in the margins · the land has a great many things still unnamed

What happens to a charm

The scribes read every card. Some charms find their way into the margins, the almanac or the lanes, credited only as you ask; none is promised, and no one is ever named in the book.

No name or email is asked: the card wants only your words. Cards the scribes do not keep become counts after three days, with no words left in them. The post says the rest on its privacy page.
